All the roads within Kruger National Park including tourist roads, management roads, firebreaks and disused roads. The roads in the 2016 version has been refined from the previous 1:50 000 scale to about 1: 5 000 scale using the 2011 &amp; 2018 aerial imagery.

Access definitions:

  • Private - roads that are accessable to staff and authorised guests only

  • Public - roads that are accessable to staff and all guests

  • Staff - roads that are accessable to staff only

Surface definitions:

  • Tar - sealed roads that provide a good all weather surface

  • Graded - unsealed roads that are regularly maintained and are suitable for sedan vehicles

  • Gravel - unsealed roads that have only basic surface maintenance done. High clearence vehicles may be needed

  • Two spoor - unsealed roads that are not maintained. High clearence vehicles needed at a minimum, but 4x4 capability may be needed in places.

  • Disused - unsealed roads that are no longer in regular use and may be overgrown and washed away in places.

  • Rehab - roads that have been permanently closed and actively rehabilitated
