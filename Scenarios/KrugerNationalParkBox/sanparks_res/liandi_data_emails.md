### Oct 4, 2021
I am going to start sending you the datasets for KNP in separate emails.
Herewith attached is the KNP gate quota numbers (daily quotas) which represent the limits placed on the number of day visitors entering KNP. 
 
It would be important to note the following:
1. the quotas apply only to day visitors and
2. the quotas exclude day visitors that enter the park on OSVs (open safari vehicles) as they are not subject to the gate quotas. This has been discussed many times at various meetings and is something that needs to be revisited. The fact that there are no gate quotas for OSVs have led to a large increase in OSV vehicles in the park in recent pre-pandemic years. While the OSV operators bring in a lot of revenues for Kruger (traditionally the majority of their guests are international visitors), it has impacted negatively on the self-drive visitor’s experience. This negative impact can be ascribed to:
a. OSV guides sharing the location of wildlife sightings with each other, causing traffic jams (and speeding to sightings)
b. Selfish OSV driver behaviour – sometimes purposefully blocking sightings for an extended time because they believe their guests pay a premium and expect to see iconic mammals; 
c. and other times just the mere presence of too many of these larger (and higher) vehicles can spoil the experience for visitors in normal sedan cars. This had led to many visitor complaints.
